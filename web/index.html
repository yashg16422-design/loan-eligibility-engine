26 lenders, across bank sites, BankBazaar and Paisabazaar

Every rule our AI takes from a lender's page must quote the sentence it came from. Code checks the quote against the page. If it can't, that rule never rules anyone out.

Work Experience: 2 yearsverifiedICICI Bank, icici.bank.in
Total 3 years of experience, 1 year in current employmentverifiedRBL Bank, via paisabazaar.com
Work experience: Minimum 1 yearverifiedTata Capital, via paisabazaar.com
robots.txt, always

If a site says no, or shows a CAPTCHA, we skip it. HDFC and Kotak's own sites are read through aggregators instead.

No quote, no rejection

A rule the AI can't quote from the page never rules anyone out. It becomes "may qualify".

Only your inbox

Emails go only to the address you give us. Never to a list.

Indicative, not an offer

We show eligibility from published criteria. The lender makes the decision.
